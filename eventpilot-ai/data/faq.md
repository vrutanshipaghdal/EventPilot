# EventPilot FAQ

## How to book
Open an event, pick your seats on the seat map (up to 10 seats per booking), enter your primary contact details and the names of each attendee, and confirm. Each attendee receives their own downloadable e-ticket with a unique entry QR code.

## Multiple tickets and attendees
When booking multiple seats (e.g. 2 or more tickets), you can enter individual attendee names for each ticket. Every attendee gets an individual ticket pass with their name, seat number, and a unique verifiable QR code. You can download each ticket separately or download all tickets together in a ZIP file.

## Seat prices
Rows A and B are premium seats and cost 50% more than standard seats. Free events have no price for any seat.

## Finding my ticket
Open the "My Bookings" page and enter your booking ID or the email you booked with. You can download the ticket again from there.

## Cancellation and refunds
Cancellations are free up to 48 hours before the event start. Within 48 hours, a 50% refund is given. Free events can be cancelled anytime. Contact the organiser at support@eventpilot.example to cancel.

## Payment
This is a demo checkout, so no real money is taken. A real payment gateway such as Razorpay can be added later.

## Entry rules
Carry a government photo ID and show the QR code on your ticket. Gates open 30 minutes before the start time. Outside food and drinks are not allowed at indoor venues.

## Children and groups
Children under 5 enter free but need a seat-less pass. Groups of 10 or more should contact the organiser for a discount.

## Accessibility
All venues are wheelchair accessible. Tell the organiser in advance if you need assistance.

## Rescheduling
If an event is rescheduled, all bookings move to the new date automatically. If you cannot attend the new date you get a full refund.
